Percentage calculator formulas and examples

Use this percentage calculator to find a percentage of a number, work out what percentage one value represents of another, compare an old value with a new value, or increase and decrease a value by a chosen percentage.

Formula: Percent of a number = (percentage ÷ 100) × number. Percentage change = ((new value − old value) ÷ old value) × 100. A positive change means an increase; a negative change means a decrease.
